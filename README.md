# Circle Clicker

[Play in your browser](https://redrookai.github.io/circle-clicker/)

Click the circle to earn points. The first generator earns 1 point per second. Each new generator makes every generator 50% stronger, giving total rates of 1, 3, 6.75 and 13.5 points per second for the first four. The shop shows the extra output from your next purchase. Buy Click Power upgrades to earn more points per click. Unlock a second clickable circle with a one-time 100-point purchase. Both circles share the score. The second earns 30% more per click, including after Click Power upgrades (1.3 instead of 1, or 2.6 instead of 2). Reloading resets progress.

Standalone HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open `index.html` locally to play or edit it.
